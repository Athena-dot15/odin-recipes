Lasagna Recipe

Description

In this classic lasagna recipe, sheets of pasta are layered with a cheesy filling, a rich meaty tomato sauce, and more cheese and then baked until bubbly and browned.

While there are a few steps, this lasagna recipe is relatively easy to make and has so much flavor.

Ingredients for 8 person

  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 125g of Butter
  • An entire box of Lasagna sheets
  • 3 yellow Onions
  • 70g of shredded cheese
  • 1 Carrot
  • 800g of Tomato Paste
  • 100g of Flour
  • 20cl of Red Wine
  • 2 cloves of Garlic
  • 600g of meat
  • 125g of Parmesan
  • 1L of milk

Steps

  1. Cut the onions and the garlic in small pieces, and caramelise it in olive oil.
  2. Cut the carrot and add it with the meat, marinate everything together.
  3. After a few minutes add the red wine. Let it cook until it fully evaporates
  4. Add the tomato paste, 25cl of water and herbs to the mixture. Add salt and pepper on top and let it marinate for 45mins.
  5. Homemade Bechamel:
    1. Melt 100g of butter
    2. Add flour to the butter and mix them while cooking them until you get a smooth cream.
    3. Add milk to the cream little by little.
    4. Mix until the cream gets thick.
    5. Add salt and pepper, let it cook for 5 more min. Then keep it on the side.
  6. Preheat the oven at 200°C. Oil the lasagna plate. put a thin layer of bechamel, then lasagna sheets, bolognaise, bechamel and then parmesan. Repeat the steps about 3 times.
  7. On the last layer of lasagna only put bechamel and shredder cheese. Spread butter on top.
  8. Put in the oven for 25 min
  9. ENJOY!
