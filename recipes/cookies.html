Cookies Recipe

Description

This is the best chocolate chip cookies recipe ever! No funny ingredients, no chilling time, etc. Just a simple, straightforward, amazingly delicious, doughy yet still fully cooked, chocolate chip cookie that turns out perfectly every single time!

Ingredients for 6 people

  • 1 egg
  • 85g of butter
  • 100g of chocolate chips
  • 1 tsp of baking soda
  • 85g of sugar
  • 150g of flour
  • half a tsp of salt

Steps

  1. Let the butter soften outside of the fridge. Then in a bowl mix it with the sugar.
  2. Add the egg.
  3. Pour the flour little by little, with the baking soda, the salt and the chocolate chips. Mix well.
  4. With two tbsp, or with the hand. Make little balls with the dough. Make sure to separate them because they are going to extend during the baking.
  5. Put them in the oven for 8 to 10 min at 180°C. Take them out when the edges start to get brown.
